Home Project: Murphy Bed

A space-saving wall-fold bed — designed and built for structural integrity, smooth daily operation, and clean aesthetic integration with the room.

Overview

Space Optimisation & Furniture Design

The bed folds neatly against the wall while remaining strong and safe for everyday use. The project combined woodworking, mechanical spring support selection, and careful ergonomic design to ensure smooth, reliable operation over time.

Key Contributions & Learnings

  • Designing for compactness, stability, and ease of use
  • Material selection — wood types, hardware, and spring supports
  • Cutting, assembly, and mechanical folding mechanism
  • Ergonomic considerations for daily use
  • Structural safety analysis and validation
  • Problem-solving for smooth operation
  • Build documentation and visual presentation
