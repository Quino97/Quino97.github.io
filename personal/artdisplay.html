Display Shelving — Dream Press

Blackened sheet steel display shelves designed for Riso-printed zine display in a printing shopfront — designed for sheet metal bending and easy wall installation.

Overview

Sheet Metal Retail Display

Designed for Dream Press, a Riso printing studio. The shelves needed to display zines attractively while being practical to manufacture and install. Blackened sheet steel was selected for its clean industrial aesthetic that complements the print studio environment.

All bending radii and flange dimensions were designed to standard sheet metal bending practices, with engineering drawings produced for the fabricator.

What I Did

  • Concept design and ideation
  • Detailed design for sheet metal bending
  • CAD modelling in SolidWorks
  • Material selection — blackened sheet steel
  • Liaison with sheet metal fabricators
  • Engineering drawings production
  • Installation planning
