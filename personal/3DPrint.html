Overview

Additive Manufacturing — Design to Print

I have fully disassembled and reassembled FDM printers multiple times, repaired faulty components, and optimised print settings for a wide range of materials, geometries and orientations.

Featured projects include a fully-printed phone tripod (only two pen springs as hardware), research components for polishing experiments, high-resolution miniatures, and a range of home organisation pieces.
