Home Project: Outdoor Couches

Designing and building a set of durable, comfortable outdoor couches — weather-resistant materials, modular assembly, and ergonomic seating for outdoor use.

Overview

Home Outdoor Furniture Build

The couches were designed and built from scratch with a focus on outdoor durability and modular construction. Different finishing and weatherproofing techniques were explored to extend the lifespan of the timber in an outdoor environment.

Key Contributions & Learnings

  • Designing for outdoor durability and weather resistance
  • Material selection — treated wood, hardware, and finishes
  • Assembly and joinery for modular construction
  • Ergonomic seating geometry and comfort design
  • Weatherproofing and finishing techniques
  • Build process documentation and iteration
