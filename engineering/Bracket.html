Yaw, Roll & Telescopic Bracket Hook

Custom multi-axis bracket system with secure locking and precise motion control for modular industrial applications.

Overview

Custom Mechanical Engineering Solution

Designed and built a bracket system featuring yaw, roll, and telescopic motion axes. Includes M8 and M12 locking bolt mechanisms for repeatable, safe positioning across modular installation scenarios.

What I Did

  • Design of multi-axis bracket system (yaw, roll, telescopic)
  • CAD modelling and dimensioning in SolidWorks
  • Mechanical strength, fatigue and stress analysis
  • Locking mechanism design with M8 and M12 bolts
  • Prototyping and assembly
  • Testing for repeatable motion and safe operation
  • Design optimisation for manufacturability
  • Project documentation and client communication
