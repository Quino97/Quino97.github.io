ZeroG — Modular Tattoo Accessory Holder

A wearable, modular arm-mounted device for tattoo artists — holding ink, needles, hygiene accessories, phone, and smartwatch in a compact form factor.

Overview

Wearable Product Design

ZeroG fits over a tattoo artist's arm and uses cantilever and annular snap-fit clip systems to enable a fully modular design. Mass was minimised throughout by optimising wall thickness and using cutout geometry. Every component was designed for FDM 3D printing.

The logo concept — an ode to tattooing in zero gravity — was created in Adobe Illustrator.

What I Did

  • Concept sketches and ideation
  • Product design and modular system architecture
  • CAD modelling in SolidWorks
  • Snap-fit mechanism design (cantilever & annular)
  • FDM 3D printing and prototyping
  • Brand logo design in Adobe Illustrator
  • Engineering drawings
  • Documentation and presentation
