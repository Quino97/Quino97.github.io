Vehicle Polisher

A final-year product design project — a compact vehicle polisher developed entirely in SolidWorks, focusing on the full product design process from concept to detailed design.

Overview

Final Year Product Design

Bevel gears translate rotational motion through 90°, while multiple ball bearings distribute load to the frame and away from the motor shaft. The design prioritises ergonomic grip, minimal mass, and ease of manufacture.

Wall thicknesses were carefully optimised to reduce mass without compromising structural integrity. A full load analysis validated the selected bearing configuration.

What I Did

  • Concept generation and elimination process
  • Full product design workflow
  • CAD modelling in SolidWorks
  • Bevel gear and bearing selection
  • Load analysis and structural validation
  • Ergonomic and mass optimisation
  • Documentation and detailed design report
